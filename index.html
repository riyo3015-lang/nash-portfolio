About Me

AI Engineer & AgriTech Specialist

I'm Nash Nauri, a final-year BCA (Artificial Intelligence) student based in Bangalore, India. I'm passionate about building technology that makes a real-world difference — especially at the intersection of AI and agriculture.

Over the past 3+ years I've immersed myself in deep learning, computer vision, and full-stack AI development. My flagship project, AgriTech Pro, uses CNN-based image classification to detect fruit crop diseases with ~95% accuracy — helping farmers catch infections early and protect their livelihoods.

When I'm not training models or writing Python, you'll find me exploring new research papers, contributing to open-source projects, or brainstorming the next thing I want to build. I'm currently seeking internship and full-time roles where I can apply AI to meaningful problems.
